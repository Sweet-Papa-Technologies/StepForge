# StepForge Flow v1.4 — model card

Trained 2026-11-15. Spec: `stepforge_neural_architecture_spec.md`. Codec 1.0.
This is the model the StepForge desktop app ships and uses. License:
`LICENSE.md` (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); credits: `CREDITS.csv`.

| | |
|---|---|
| Stage A — RhythmNet | 6-layer bidirectional Conformer, d=256, AdaLN-Zero conditioning, 9.64M params |
| Stage B — ChoreoNet | 8-layer masked discrete diffusion transformer, d=320, 15.22M params |
| Files | `stage_a_rhythm.onnx` (38.8 MB), `stage_b_choreo.onnx` (61.1 MB), int8 variants (13.7 + 16.2 MB), `phrase_index.npz` (style retrieval, 16,064 phrases), `CREDITS.csv` |
| Runtime | numpy + scipy + simfile + onnxruntime; no PyTorch |
| Stems | **not** trained with Demucs stems (`trained_with_stems: false`); the stem channels are zero |

## Training data

254 songs (1,066 dance-single charts, about 8.2 hours of audio) from:

| Source | Songs | Charts |
|---|---:|---:|
| StepMix 1.0, 2, 3, 4 and 5 (community contest packs) | 235 | 979 |
| OutFox Serenity, Volumes 1–3 | 17 | 77 |
| StepMania 5 default songs | 2 | 10 |

Every song is listed in `CREDITS.csv` with its artist, step artists, pack,
audio and chart license, the attribution text and where it came from.

**Licenses vary by song.** 218 of the 254 are StepMix contest entries whose audio
and steps are both under the StepMix contest grant; the rest carry Creative Commons licenses (CC-BY,
CC-BY-SA, some non-commercial variants, CC Sampling Plus) or the OutFox
Serenity free-content terms. The songs were checked for redistribution
permission; most licenses do not address machine-learning training one way or
the other. The set contains no charts generated by StepForge itself.

The songs went through the engine's quality gates: a sync check, BPM
50–320, at most 8 stops, 4/4 only, notes on the 48-tick grid, and a foot-model
discomfort ceiling. 254 of the 387 audited songs passed (the StepMix packs use
many tempo gimmicks). One song that shares audio with a held-out benchmark song
was removed. Training used 227 songs (950 charts); 27 (116 charts) were held
for validation.

## Results

Benchmark: 180 community songs with human charts that this model never saw,
charted blind from audio and compared with the human chart of the same song and
difficulty. "smlab" is the strongest open-source auto-charter we tested (others:
NoteSight, ITGPT, DDC, AutoStepper). "Human" is how closely a second human
charter's chart of the same song matches the first.

| 180 held-out songs | StepForge Flow v1.4 | smlab | human |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Step-placement F1 (±20 ms) | 0.656 | 0.674 | ≈ 0.75 |
| … Beginner / Easy | 0.519 / 0.657 | 0.587 / 0.687 | |
| … Medium | 0.689 | 0.689 | |
| … Hard / Challenge | 0.714 / 0.726 | 0.703 / 0.712 | |
| Looks like a human chart (12 statistics) | 98% | 90% | 94% |
| Unforced double-steps, share of notes | 0.01% | 1.04% | 0.35% |
| Charts with no step the foot model rates forbidden | 100% | 84% | 78% |
| Density follows song energy (Spearman ρ) | 0.674 | 0.446 | 0.417 |

On placement v1.4 is level with smlab (−0.017, not significant), ahead of it
on Hard and Challenge and behind it on Beginner. Playability does not depend on
the training data: those guarantees come from the decoder and foot model. We
could not check whether the 180 test songs appear in smlab's training data.

Validation (27 songs): Stage A precision at matched density 0.780, AP 0.793
(best epoch 44 of 100, regularized variant: dropout 0.3, weight decay 0.05);
Stage B cross-entropy 1.418, 48% accuracy with half the notes masked (epoch 120
of 140).

## Speed (Apple Silicon laptop CPU, fp32)

Features 0.2–0.7 s per song; then 0.15–0.3 s per chart for a 53 s song, up to
about 2.3 s for a 3-minute Challenge chart. Re-rolling 8 bars: ~0.2 s.

## Known limits

- **Beginner and Easy are the weak tiers.** The engine checks whether the beat
  grid sits half a beat off by asking the model, and this model answers less
  reliably than one trained on more data would: the grid is wrong on about 1 song in 8, and
  those songs' Beginner and Easy charts land between the beats. It also writes
  more notes than human charts at those two tiers (1.65× and 1.40×).
- Stage A's validation precision plateaus near 0.78: about a fifth of a human
  chart's notes are choices another charter would have made differently.
- Trained on dance-single 4/4 charts without gimmick timing; constant-BPM
  inference only (what the engine's DSP provides).
- No Demucs stems; the stem path exists in the feature layout and is untested
  in training.
- The phrase index uses the built-in feature embedder, not EmbeddingGemma 2.
